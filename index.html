Privacy Policy — Hurtz

App: Hurtz · Provider: Bekodemy · Last updated: 29 October 2026

Hurtz ("the app") is a tone and noise generator published by Bekodemy. Your privacy is straightforward here because the app collects nothing and sends nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, share, or transmit any personal data. It has no server and no Internet permission.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting, or advertising.
  • No network access — the app has no Internet permission and never sends data anywhere.
  • No microphone, camera, location, contacts, or file access.

What stays on your device

The app stores these items only on your device, in its private app storage: your settings (for example frequency, waveform, volume, theme, timer length and the background-play switch) and your favorites, including the optional short notes you type. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own backup may include them, for example Android Auto Backup or an iCloud backup of your iPhone, depending on your account settings; the app itself sends nothing. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them from the device.

The app also stores one local flag that says whether Hurtz Pro is active. It holds no name, no email, no payment data and no purchase history.

  • Home-screen widget (Android): if you add the Hurtz widget, it shows your current sound and the names (notes) of up to three favorites on your home screen. The widget keeps its own private copy on the device. Tapping it opens the app; it never starts sound.
  • Copy and paste of favorites (optional): the app uses the system clipboard only when you tap the copy or paste button for favorites. It does not read the clipboard at any other time.
  • Support and rate links (optional): if you tap a support or rate link, your device opens your mail app or the app store. The app itself sends nothing; what happens next is governed by that app's own policy.
  • Info source links (optional): they open in your browser. Hurtz sends nothing.

All audio is generated on your device. When the setting "Keep playing in background" is on, sound you started can keep playing while you use other apps or the screen is locked.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13 and later): the app asks for notification permission only to show the playback control (Stop and the sound name) on your lock screen and in the notification shade while a sound you started is playing. It never sends other notifications. If you say no, sound still plays.
  • Background play (Android): to keep sound playing in the background, Android gives the app a media playback service and a wake lock. They run only while sound plays.
  • iOS and Windows: the app asks for no runtime permissions. On iOS it uses the audio background mode so your sound can continue when the phone is locked.

The app has no other special permissions; it produces sound through your device's normal audio output.

Hurtz Pro subscription (optional)

Hurtz is free to use. You can choose to buy Hurtz Pro, an optional subscription.

  • The store handles everything. Google Play (on Android) or the Apple App Store (on iOS) handles the purchase, the payment and the billing. We receive no payment data and no account. We do not see your name, email, card or purchase history.
  • No account in the app. The app still has no accounts and no sign-in. The app stores only a local flag on your device that says whether Pro is active.
  • No extra permission. The app itself requests no Internet permission. The store app on your device does the billing.
  • Free trial. A new subscriber can get a 14-day free trial. The trial turns into a paid subscription when it ends, unless you cancel before then. Each store account gets the trial once.
  • Renewal. A monthly or yearly subscription renews by itself at the end of each period until you cancel. A lifetime purchase is paid once and does not renew.
  • Cancel or manage. Cancel in the settings of the store: Subscriptions in Google Play, or Subscriptions in your Apple ID settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds follow the rules of the store that sold the subscription.
  • Restore. The "Restore purchases" button asks the store whether your account owns Pro. No other data is sent by the app.
  • The store's own privacy policy governs the data it collects when you buy.

Children and target audience

Hurtz is intended for people aged 13 and older, because loud or sustained tones need careful volume control. It is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. It contains no ads and no user-generated content that is shared with others. The optional Pro subscription is sold only through the store, which sets its own age rules.

Third-party components

The app uses open-source software components (for example the SoLoud audio engine) and the Inter and JetBrains Mono fonts to generate sound and draw text on the device. These run locally and do not collect or transmit data. Their licenses are viewable in the app under Settings and About → Open source licenses.
