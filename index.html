Privacy Policy

Provider: Bekodemy  ·  Last updated: 23 July 2026

Hurtz (“the app”) is a simple audio tone generator published by Bekodemy. Your privacy is straightforward here because the app collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No network access — the app has no Internet permission and never sends data anywhere.
  • No microphone, camera, location, contacts, or file access.

All audio is generated on your device and plays only while you use the app.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. It only produces sound through your device’s normal audio output.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, purchases, or user-generated content.

Third-party components

The app uses open-source software components (e.g. the SoLoud audio engine) to generate sound on the device. These run locally and do not collect or transmit data. Their licenses are viewable in the app under About → View licenses.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
