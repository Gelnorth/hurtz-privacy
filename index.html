Privacy Policy — Hurtz

App: Hurtz · Provider: Bekodemy · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Hurtz ("the app") is a tone and noise generator by Bekodemy. The app has no account, no analytics and no server of ours. The free version offers one optional ad that you can choose to watch; it is provided by Google AdMob. Hurtz Pro is an optional purchase handled by the store. This policy explains what that means for your data.

What we collect

We do not collect your personal data, and we have no server that receives it. Your settings and favorites stay on your device. The optional ad and the store purchase are the exceptions: Google (AdMob, Google Play) handles them, not us.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash reporting of our own.
  • No microphone, camera, location, contacts, or file access.
  • Ads (free Android version only, optional): see Advertising.
  • Purchases: see Hurtz Pro.

Advertising (free version, Android)

The free version on Android offers one optional ad: you can choose to watch it to unlock Pro features for 8 hours (up to 3 times a day). There is no banner, and no ad plays unless you tap that button. The ads come from Google AdMob.

  • When an ad loads: only after you tap the button, the Google Mobile Ads software asks Google for an ad over the Internet. Google receives data such as your IP address, your device type and operating system version, the app name and version, your advertising ID, and how the ad performed (shown, completed, closed). Google uses it to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, under Google's Privacy Policy. We see only summed-up ad performance in our AdMob account, not data about you personally.
  • When the app starts (free version): the app starts Google's consent software (User Messaging Platform) to check whether a consent form is needed. Only if consent allows ads does it also start the Google Mobile Ads software. Both can contact Google at that time, before you tap anything, for example to check your consent status and to get settings. No ad is requested at that time.
  • Your choice: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows Google's consent form at start, before the ad software starts. If you do not agree, no ad is requested and the ad button says that no ad is available. To change your choice, open Settings and About → Ad privacy choices (this row appears only where the form is required).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • Hurtz Pro: if Hurtz Pro is active on your device, the app does not start the consent software or the ad software and requests no ads. One exception: on a new install or a new device the app cannot know that you own Pro until Google Play answers (normally within seconds), so the consent check may run once before that.
  • Windows and iOS: the app shows no ads and runs no ad software.

Hurtz Pro (optional purchase)

Hurtz Pro is optional: a monthly plan, a yearly plan with a 14-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

  • The store handles it: Google Play on Android (the Apple App Store on iOS where Pro is sold) handles the purchase, the payment, the billing and refunds. We receive no payment data and we do not see your name, email, card or purchase history. The app has no account.
  • The app asks the store only whether your account owns Pro, when it starts and when you tap "Restore purchases", and keeps the answer as a local flag. If you are offline, it uses the last answer.
  • Cancel or manage: in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, or in Settings and About → Manage subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • The store's own privacy policy governs the data it collects when you buy.

What stays on your device

The app stores these items only on your device, in its private app storage: your settings (for example frequency, waveform, volume, theme, timer length and the background-play switch); your favorites, including the optional short notes you type; one local flag that says whether Hurtz Pro is active; and, if you watched an ad, the end time of the unlock, how many ads you watched today and the time of the last one. They hold no name, no email, no payment data and no purchase history. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own backup may include them, for example Android Auto Backup or an iCloud backup of your iPhone, depending on your account settings; the app itself sends nothing. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them from the device.

  • Home-screen widget (Android): if you add the Hurtz widget, it shows your current sound and the names (notes) of up to three favorites on your home screen. The widget keeps its own private copy on the device. Tapping it opens the app; it never starts sound.
  • Copy and paste of favorites (optional): the app uses the system clipboard only when you tap the copy or paste button for favorites. It does not read the clipboard at any other time.
  • Support and rate links (optional): if you tap a support or rate link, your device opens your mail app or the app store. The app itself sends nothing; what happens next is governed by that app's own policy.
  • Info source links (optional): they open in your browser and lead to third-party websites that Bekodemy does not control. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Hurtz sends nothing when you tap them.

All audio is generated on your device. When the setting "Keep playing in background" is on, sound you started can keep playing while you use other apps or the screen is locked.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13 and later): the app asks for notification permission only to show the playback control (Stop and the sound name) on your lock screen and in the notification shade while a sound you started is playing. It never sends other notifications. If you say no, sound still plays.
  • Background play (Android): to keep sound playing in the background, Android gives the app a media playback service and a wake lock. They run only while sound plays.
  • iOS and Windows: the app asks for no runtime permissions. On iOS it uses the audio background mode so your sound can continue when the phone is locked.

The Android version also declares the Internet and network state permissions, to load the optional ad and to talk to Google Play for purchases; the advertising ID permission, so an ad can be shown and measured; and the Google Play billing permission, to sell Hurtz Pro. The app has no other special permissions; it produces sound through your device's normal audio output.

Children and target audience

Hurtz is intended for people aged 13 and older, because loud or sustained tones need careful volume control. It is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. In the ad settings, the app does not mark ad requests as child-directed or as coming from users under the age of consent (both are left unspecified), because the app is for ages 13 and up. It limits ad content to the PG rating or milder. The optional Pro purchase is sold only through the store, which sets its own age rules.

Third-party components

The app uses open-source software (for example the SoLoud audio engine) and the Inter and JetBrains Mono fonts to generate sound and draw text on the device. These run locally and do not collect or transmit data. The Google Mobile Ads SDK, the Google consent form and the Google Play billing software connect to Google, as described under Advertising and Hurtz Pro. Their licenses are viewable under Settings and About → Open source licenses.
